# Illustrative 360° panorama image provenance

## Active native 4K collection

The active studio collection has 40 native 4K panoramas: 36 new `gpt-image-2` concepts at 3840 × 1920, generated through the bundled image CLI/API, and four preserved original bedroom visualisations at 4096 × 2048. There are three generated views per category; Bedrooms has seven views including its four originals. The former 1774 × 887 concepts below are replaced and are no longer active studio designs.

The [exact generation jobs](../content/panorama-4k-jobs.jsonl) preserve every current prompt and its requested native dimensions, high quality, WebP format and compression setting of 95. The [visual review log](../content/panorama-4k-review.json) records source paths, SHA-256 hashes, decoded dimensions, approval decisions and specific projection limitations. See [generation and import details](panorama-4k-plan.md) and the [original bedroom source audit](legacy-tour-resolution.md).

Raw API outputs use `output/imagegen/panoramas-4k/<id>.webp`. Approved full images are copied byte-for-byte to `img/panoramas/4k/<id>.webp`; only the previews are downscaled, to 1200 × 600 at WebP quality 85. The active [panorama manifest](../content/panoramas.json) records selection metadata, exact prompts, model and source hashes. The build exposes presentation fields only. The completed production build passed all 28 unit/source tests and both browser suites on local port 4174, including all 40 panoramas and 12 layouts, native 4K texture dimensions, high-density rendering, selection controls and 320–1440px layouts.

The generated views are illustrative design concepts, not completed Alankaar projects, measured surveys or digital twins. The four original bedroom sources are existing visualisations, not documented client photographs. Visitors rotate at a single camera position; generated furnishing, seam or pole inconsistencies may remain.

### Archived output and inspection

Each selected historical source output is 1774 × 887 pixels, exactly 2:1. The full native image was encoded as WebP quality 89; each preview was 1200 × 600 pixels at quality 83. Pillow performed only deterministic resizing and WebP encoding, without creative retouching, compositing, projection conversion or artificial upscaling. The archived full-size assets remain in `img/panoramas/`; these entries are replaced in the active `content/panoramas.json` manifest.

All twelve earlier outputs were visually inspected for full-room coverage, equirectangular wall/ceiling curvature, stretched zenith/nadir regions and left/right continuation. Wraparound edge strips were inspected as an additional check. Small edge, pole and furnishing inconsistencies can remain in generated imagery, so these historical images are not accurate architectural measurements.
