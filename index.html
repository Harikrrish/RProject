WHO WE ARE?

Welcome to AlanKaar Home Interior, where creativity meets functionality to transform spaces into tailored sanctuaries. Founded in 2018 by Radhika, our company has dedicated itself to bringing innovative and bespoke interior design solutions to our clients. Nestled in the heart of Chennai, we are a team of passionate designers, architects, and creatives who believe in the power of design to enhance lifestyles and elevate experiences.

WHY PICK US?

We think that the best designs are both pretty and practical. They should make your life easier and make you happy just looking at them. We work closely with you because it’s your space, and it should show off your style

Clients choose us because we:

  • Making cozy and stylish spots for you and your family
  • Building places that make customers and workers happy
  • Helping you figure out the best plan from the very first idea
  • Making sure every inch of your place is used well
