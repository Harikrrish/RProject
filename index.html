Good questions to start with

A few things
you might be wondering.

Can you work with my budget?+

Tell us what you have in mind, along with your floor plan and the rooms you want to work on. We can discuss priorities and material options, then prepare a scope to review. The final cost depends on your measurements, finishes and the work involved.

Do I need to design the whole house?+

No. You can start with a kitchen, a bedroom, storage or a specific renovation. We’ll help you consider how that work fits with the rest of your home.

Can I see the design before work begins?+

We offer 3D visualisations and virtual tours to help you understand the proposed layout. Discuss which drawings and views are included when we agree on your scope.

How long will my interiors take?+

That depends on the scope, site condition, material availability and design approvals. We’ll discuss a schedule for your project once those details are clear. Renovation can also need time for repairs before new finishes go in.

Where in Chennai do you work?+

Our studio is in Thuraipakkam and we serve Chennai. Share your property location when you contact us so we can confirm site visits and project availability.
